# Small Systems Lab — PhD-Level Research Architecture

This directory is the research architecture for Small Systems Lab: a dossier of theory,
methods, protocols, starter code, schemas, curriculum, evaluation tools, and
implementation infrastructure, meant to help a university reviewer, faculty mentor,
funder, or collaborator understand that the lab is not a vague idea.

It is not a pitch on its own — it lives inside the main
[Small Systems Lab hub repository](../README.md), alongside the hub's method
documentation (`../docs/`) and the six SSL branches (Omoluabi, Earth Sensors Lab, Echo,
Ounjẹ, Umada, Accessible by Design). The public-facing pages built from this material are
at [`../research/`](../research/index.html).

## What this build adds

- theoretical framework
- literature review
- research questions
- methodology
- ethics and IRB protocol templates
- evaluation framework
- case study templates
- reproducibility plan
- data management plan
- publication strategy
- code starters (Arduino, p5.js)
- JSON schemas
- funding and equipment planning

## Core research claim

Technology education should prepare students not only to make things, but to understand
what those things do to people, communities, institutions, and public life.

## Status

This is a founding research build. Case studies, literature citations, and institutional
partnerships referenced here are proposals and candidates for future development, not
completed or confirmed work — see `15_CASE_STUDY_ARCHITECTURE.md` and
`03_LITERATURE_REVIEW.md`. It should be reviewed, extended, and tested before being
treated as final institutional material.
