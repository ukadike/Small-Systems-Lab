# 13 — Publication Strategy

## Publication formats

Small Systems Lab can produce:

- technical reports
- design case studies
- conference papers
- workshop papers
- open educational resources
- public essays
- project documentation
- exhibition materials
- grant reports

## Potential venues and communities

- accessibility and HCI conferences
- design research venues
- digital humanities venues
- journalism innovation forums
- learning sciences communities
- public-interest technology networks
- art and technology festivals
- disability studies forums

## Publication pipeline

1. Studio project
2. Documentation review
3. Accessibility review
4. Case study draft
5. Internal critique
6. External collaborator review
7. Public release
8. Submission or presentation

## Authorship

Student authorship should be taken seriously.

When students contribute original research, design, code, documentation, or analysis, they should be credited appropriately.
