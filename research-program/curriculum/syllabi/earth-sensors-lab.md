# Syllabus — Earth Sensors Lab

## Course description

Students build accessible environmental sensing systems using Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, p5.js, and open hardware.

## Weekly schedule

1. Environmental observation
2. Arduino sensor basics
3. Soil and water
4. Air and weather
5. Data logging
6. Visualization
7. Sonification
8. Tactile data
9. Renewable power
10. Enclosures and field deployment
11. Community science
12. Accessibility testing
13. Documentation
14. Showcase
