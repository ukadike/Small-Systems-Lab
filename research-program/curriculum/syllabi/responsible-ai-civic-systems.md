# Syllabus — Responsible AI and Civic Systems

## Course description

Students design human-governed AI tools for journalism, civic information, accessibility, and public trust.

## Weekly schedule

1. AI as public infrastructure
2. Journalism, evidence, and sourcing
3. Human-in-the-loop systems
4. Provenance and citations
5. Misinformation and contradiction
6. Accessibility in AI interfaces
7. Multilingual public communication
8. Civic datasets
9. Risk and harm review
10. Prototype sprint
11. Usability testing
12. Documentation
13. Public demo
14. Reflection
