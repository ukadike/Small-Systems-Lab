# 06 — Research Programs

## An interdisciplinary research ecosystem

Contemporary challenges—including accessibility, artificial intelligence, environmental change, misinformation, public trust, and equitable access to technology—cannot be effectively addressed within a single discipline.

Small Systems Lab creates an interdisciplinary environment where students, researchers, artists, journalists, engineers, designers, educators, and community partners collaboratively develop technologies that strengthen people, communities, and public life.

## 6.1 Accessible Civic Intelligence / Omoluabi

### Purpose

Research responsible AI, journalism, civic technology, multilingual communication, accessibility, transparency, provenance, public trust, and democratic participation.

### Research questions

- How can AI support civic understanding without replacing human judgment?
- How can provenance and source transparency become accessible interface features?
- How can civic information systems serve multilingual and disabled publics?

### Methods

- interface prototyping
- source provenance modeling
- civic scenario testing
- accessibility review
- editorial workflow analysis

### Example projects

- AI-assisted civic briefing system
- accessible public meeting summarizer
- local news verification dashboard
- blind-editor accessible newsroom interface

## 6.2 Earth Sensors Lab

### Purpose

Research accessible environmental observation using Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, and open hardware.

### Research questions

- How can low-cost sensing support community science?
- How can environmental data become accessible through sound, touch, and plain language?
- How can students learn climate and systems thinking through physical computing?

### Methods

- Arduino prototyping
- field observation
- data visualization
- sonification
- community science workshops
- accessibility review

### Example projects

- Arduino soil moisture observatory
- accessible classroom weather station
- sonified plant sensor
- tactile climate dashboard

## 6.3 Accessible by Design

### Purpose

Research accessibility as a creative methodology rather than a compliance requirement.

### Research questions

- What design practices emerge when accessibility is present from the first sketch?
- How do students understand access differently after testing with real users?
- How can accessibility documentation become part of creative practice?

### Methods

- accessibility audits
- user testing
- inclusive design workshops
- screen reader testing
- keyboard-only testing
- cognitive walkthroughs

## 6.4 Physical Computing and Creative Technology Studio

### Purpose

Research tangible interaction through electronics, fabrication, robotics, embedded systems, creative coding, interactive media, and computational art.

### Research questions

- How can physical computing support embodied learning?
- How can tangible systems become more accessible?
- How can low-power and repairable systems inform public-interest technology?

## 6.5 Learning Systems and STEAM Education

### Purpose

Research inclusive creative technology education for learners of all ages.

### Research questions

- How can Arduino and p5.js support accessible STEAM learning?
- How can teacher tools reduce barriers to creative technology education?
- What does inclusive informal learning look like in museums, libraries, and community spaces?

## 6.6 Speculative Systems Research / UMADA

### Purpose

Research world-building, narrative prototyping, futures thinking, disability futures, civic imagination, ethics, and governance.

### Research questions

- How can speculative fiction function as a research environment?
- How can narrative prototypes help students evaluate emerging technologies?
- How can future worlds reveal present-day assumptions about disability, governance, and infrastructure?
