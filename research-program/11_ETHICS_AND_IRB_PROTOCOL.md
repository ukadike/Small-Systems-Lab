# 11 — Ethics and IRB Protocol Template

## Purpose

This document provides a template for ethical review of Small Systems Lab research involving students, community partners, disabled participants, interviews, surveys, observations, or usability testing.

## Human subjects caution

Any research intended to contribute to generalizable knowledge and involving human participants may require institutional review.

Before collecting data from students, disabled participants, community members, or partners, consult the relevant institutional review process.

## Consent principles

Participants should understand:

- what the project is
- what data will be collected
- how data will be used
- whether participation is optional
- how to withdraw
- what risks exist
- how privacy will be protected
- whether outputs will be public

## Accessibility principles

Consent materials should be accessible:

- plain language
- screen-reader friendly
- large print option
- captions/transcripts for video or audio
- translation where appropriate
- time for questions

## Risk categories

Potential risks include:

- privacy exposure
- disability disclosure
- community misrepresentation
- accessibility burden
- emotional discomfort
- data misuse
- extractive research dynamics

## Mitigation

- collect only necessary data
- anonymize where possible
- allow participants to review representations
- avoid publicizing sensitive access needs
- credit collaborators appropriately
- compensate participants where possible
- document limitations honestly

## Minimal protocol outline

1. Project title
2. Research question
3. Participants
4. Recruitment
5. Consent process
6. Data collected
7. Data storage
8. Risks and mitigations
9. Accessibility accommodations
10. Public outputs
11. Withdrawal process
12. Contact person
