# 15 — Case Study Architecture

## Purpose

Case studies turn the founder's experience and student projects into research evidence.

They should analyze what was learned, not merely list accomplishments.

## Case study template

1. Context
2. Problem
3. Stakeholders
4. Methods
5. Prototype or intervention
6. Accessibility considerations
7. Outcomes
8. Limitations
9. Lessons for Small Systems Lab
10. Future research questions

## Candidate founder case studies

These should be developed carefully and honestly:

- Eyebeam artist initiatives and inclusion
- Lincoln Center digital accessibility fellowship
- Processing Foundation accessibility syllabus and mentorship
- Journalism and newsroom systems experience
- Accessible by Design prototyping
- Earth Sensors Lab
- Omoluabi
- UMADA as speculative systems research

## Evidence standards

Each case study should distinguish:

- documented fact
- personal reflection
- design interpretation
- future proposal
