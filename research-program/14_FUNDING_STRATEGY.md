# 14 — Funding Strategy

Small Systems Lab is fundable because it sits at the intersection of urgent priorities:

- AI and society
- accessibility and disability innovation
- climate and environmental sensing
- STEM and STEAM education
- public-interest technology
- journalism and civic information
- youth education
- open-source infrastructure

## Fundable outputs

- student fellowships
- accessible Arduino curricula
- environmental sensor kits
- public-interest AI prototypes
- community workshops
- annual showcase
- research publications
- accessibility audit tools

## Three funding levels

### Level 1: Pilot

One course, materials, GitHub Pages, showcase.

### Level 2: Lab development

Student fellows, equipment, documentation, partner pilots.

### Level 3: Center formation

Multiple courses, visiting researchers, publications, multi-year partnerships.

## Funding narrative

Small Systems Lab is an investment in students who can build accessible, accountable, and socially useful systems in an era of AI, climate change, and public uncertainty.
