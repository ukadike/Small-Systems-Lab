# SSL Branches

## Omoluabi
Editorial intelligence, civic documentation, consent, provenance, public knowledge, and the reporter's notebook as future archive. First live implementation: [Omoluabi News](https://ukadike.github.io/omoluabi-news/).

## Earth Sensors Lab
Accessible environmental sensing, school-based STEAM, gardens, climate, soil, weather, sonification, and interstellar curiosity.

## AEN / African Sovereign Compute
Energy, materials, power electronics, storage, thermal systems, edge compute, and computational sovereignty. AEN develops a modular, DC-forward energy-and-compute architecture and publishes its reasoning as active public research. The working boundary is explicit: integrate mature industrial components where appropriate, while concentrating original research on modular Energy Cassettes, orchestration, graceful degradation, thermal reuse/storage, local/offline control, and African-controlled deployment pathways.

## ECHO
AI governance through accessibility, human agency, and public learning. ECHO develops Accessibility-Constrained Intelligence (ACI): the thesis that **access is a condition of correctness** and that required accessibility dimensions can function as non-compensable gates in AI evaluation and deployment. AI literacy, accessible prompt education, workshops, and creative technology learning remain part of ECHO as public-governance infrastructure.

## Umada
Speculative fiction, systems collapse, future histories, chimeras, cyborgs, ancient memory, and civic signs.

## Accessible by Design
Accessibility audits, tools, principles, curriculum, and design justice.
