# SSL Branches

Small Systems Lab connects nine branches across civic infrastructure, ecology, accessibility, governance, memory, and speculative practice. This list is also published on the [SSL home page](https://ukadike.github.io/Small-Systems-Lab/#branches) and [site map](https://ukadike.github.io/Small-Systems-Lab/site-map.html).

## Omoluabi
Editorial intelligence, civic documentation, consent, provenance, public knowledge, and the reporter's notebook as future archive. [Live site](https://ukadike.github.io/omoluabi/) · [Repository](https://github.com/ukadike/omoluabi) · First live implementation: [Omoluabi News](https://ukadike.github.io/omoluabi-news/).

## UMADA
Speculative fiction, systems collapse, future histories, language memory, chimeras, cyborgs, ancient memory, and civic signs. [Live site](https://ukadike.github.io/Umada/) · [Repository](https://github.com/ukadike/Umada).

## Accessible by Design
Accessibility audits, tools, principles, curriculum, and design justice. Accessibility is a cross-cutting requirement across SSL. [Repository](https://github.com/ukadike/accessible-by-design-prototyping).

## Earth Sensors Lab
Accessible environmental sensing, school-based STEAM, gardens, climate, soil, weather, sonification, and interstellar curiosity. [Live site](https://ukadike.github.io/Earth-Sensors-Lab/) · [Repository](https://github.com/ukadike/Earth-Sensors-Lab).

## ECHO
AI governance through accessibility, human agency, and public learning. ECHO develops Accessibility-Constrained Intelligence (ACI): the thesis that **access is a condition of correctness** and required accessibility dimensions can function as non-compensable gates in AI evaluation and deployment. [Live site](https://ukadike.github.io/Echo/) · [Repository](https://github.com/ukadike/Echo).

## AEN / African Sovereign Compute
Energy, materials, power electronics, storage, thermal systems, edge compute, and computational sovereignty. AEN develops a modular, DC-forward energy-and-compute architecture and publishes its reasoning as active public research. [Research page](https://ukadike.github.io/Small-Systems-Lab/aen-sovereign-compute/) · [Research files](https://github.com/ukadike/Small-Systems-Lab/tree/main/aen-sovereign-compute).

## Ìjóyà
Soft robotics and garden systems exploring shared function, touch, wind, and interdependence. [Repository](https://github.com/ukadike/Ijoya).

## Ìrántí
Wearable memory objects exploring how materials and personal artifacts can hold and carry memory. In development; no public site or repository yet.

## Ounjẹ
Food archive, healing recipes, Nigerian and diasporic cooking, memory, care, and cultural documentation. In development; no public site or repository yet.
