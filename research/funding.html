Small Systems Lab / Research Program

Funding Strategy

Small Systems Lab sits at the intersection of accessibility, AI, climate, journalism, STEAM education, public-interest technology, and open-source infrastructure.

Overview · Research Programs · Curriculum · Student Belonging · Funding

Three funding levels

Level 1: Pilot

One course, materials, GitHub Pages, showcase.

Level 2: Lab development

Student fellows, equipment, documentation, partner pilots.

Level 3: Center formation

Multiple courses, visiting researchers, publications, multi-year partnerships.

Fundable outputs

  • student fellowships
  • accessible Arduino curricula
  • environmental sensor kits
  • public-interest AI prototypes
  • community workshops
  • annual showcase
  • research publications
  • accessibility audit tools
