Studio promise

You do not need to adapt yourself to the lab. The lab is designed with you in mind.

Disability as knowledge

Disability is treated as a source of design knowledge, systems insight, and technological imagination.

Belonging indicators

The lab should track:

  • whether students feel safe asking for access needs
  • whether disabled students hold authorship and leadership roles
  • whether documentation is accessible
  • whether multiple forms of participation are supported
  • whether student projects reflect diverse user contexts
