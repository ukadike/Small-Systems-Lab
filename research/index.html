Research Program

A PhD-level research architecture for an accessibility-first creative technology studio: theory, methods, ethics, curriculum, evaluation, and six research programs.

Overview · Research Programs · Curriculum · Student Belonging · Funding

What this is

This is a research architecture, not only a pitch: a public dossier with theory, literature positioning, research questions, methodology, ethics and IRB templates, an evaluation framework, case study templates, a reproducibility plan, a data management plan, a publication strategy, starter code, JSON schemas, and funding and equipment planning.

Core research claim: technology education should prepare students not only to make things, but to understand what those things do to people, communities, institutions, and public life.

Research infrastructure

Arduino as backbone

Physical computing makes systems tangible, testable, and accessible. See starter code in research-program/code/arduino/ and research-program/code/p5/.

Methods and ethics

The research program includes research questions, protocols, evaluation tools, and data plans — see research-program/05_METHODOLOGY.md and research-program/11_ETHICS_AND_IRB_PROTOCOL.md.

Open-source documentation

GitHub is treated as a public research notebook and teaching platform. Full documentation starts at research-program/00_START_HERE.md.

Full documentation set

The complete research build lives in research-program/, including the theoretical framework, literature review, research questions, research programs, curriculum, student belonging, evaluation framework, ethics and IRB protocol, data management plan, publication strategy, funding strategy, case study architecture, and reproducibility plan.

Status

This is a founding research build. Case studies, literature citations, and institutional partnerships referenced in this section are proposals and candidates for future development, not completed or confirmed work. It should be reviewed, extended, and tested before being treated as final institutional material.

Small Systems Lab treats documentation, care labor, accessibility, and public memory as infrastructure.
